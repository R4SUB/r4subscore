- Clarified the package DESCRIPTION: “R4SUB” expands to “Ready for
  Submission” (previously “R for Regulatory Submission”, inconsistent
  with the rest of the ecosystem).

## r4subscore 0.1.1

- Add vignette: “Submission Confidence Index (SCI) Scoring” covering
  [`compute_pillar_scores()`](https://r4sub.github.io/r4subscore/reference/compute_pillar_scores.md),
  [`compute_sci()`](https://r4sub.github.io/r4subscore/reference/compute_sci.md),
  [`sci_explain()`](https://r4sub.github.io/r4subscore/reference/sci_explain.md),
  [`sci_sensitivity_analysis()`](https://r4sub.github.io/r4subscore/reference/sci_sensitivity_analysis.md),
  and custom weight configuration.
